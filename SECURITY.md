# AAAP v2.x — Ethical Safeguard & Anti-Misuse Protocol

> *"The methodology must be stronger than the person using it."*

Whether wielded by an individual, a journalist, an artificial intelligence, or a massive public institution, the rules and ethical boundaries apply equally to all. This protocol establishes the foundational boundaries, safeguarding mechanisms, and operational integrity of the **AAAP v2.0 (Algorithmic Accountability and Audit Protocol)** framework.

---

## 1. Separation of Documentation and Accusation
* **Golden Rule:** *"Citizen Auditing documents evidence; it does not determine guilt."*
* **Application:** An auditor/citizen is neither a prosecutor nor a judge. Their role is to impartially seal events and digital footprints with UTC timestamps. The authority to draw legal or administrative conclusions is left entirely to independent authorities.

## 2. Physical Separation of Observation and Interpretation
* **Structure:** Every report or record is divided into two distinct sections:
  * **Verified Observations:** Immutable, raw, and concrete data.
  * **Interpretations (Optional):** Analytical evaluations built upon those observations.
* **Objective:** Ensure readers can immediately distinguish between facts and assessments, protecting them from manipulation.

## 3. Explicit Definition of Misuse
* **Boundaries:** The Citizen Auditing methodology can never be used for:
  * Personal harassment or targeting
  * Coordinated spam campaigns
  * Generating misinformation
  * Creating fake or manipulated documents
* **Consequence:** Individuals who commit these violations are excluded from the AAAP ecosystem; the methodology rejects such uses entirely.

## 4. Principle of Proportionality
* **Approach:** Not every discrepancy or flaw requires a global crisis or a multi-wave campaign.
* **Guiding Question:** *"Who is the most appropriate counterpart for this issue?"* By directing complaints or notifications to the correct authority, citizens deploy their energy efficiently while preventing unnecessary burdens on institutional mechanisms.

## 5. Audit and Limitation of Artificial Intelligence
* **Core Rule:** Artificial intelligence does not generate evidence; it organizes and structures evidence.
* **Principle:** AI systems must always be reminded: *"Show your source."* Artificial intelligence can never replace human oversight and will; it is positioned strictly as a transparent assistant.

## 6. Transparent Correction & Appeal Mechanism
* **Philosophy:** The strongest methodologies are not those that claim to be infallible, but those capable of correcting their errors most swiftly and transparently.
* **Appeal & Feedback:** If any institution or individual believes that a record contains a factual error, incomplete context, or misinterpretation, they are invited to submit supporting evidence for transparent review.

## 7. Do No Harm & Minimum Necessary Disclosure
* **Do No Harm:** Citizen Auditing shall minimize unnecessary harm to individuals while maximizing factual transparency, fully aligning with human rights and research ethics.
* **Minimum Necessary Disclosure:** Only the minimum information necessary for transparency should be publicly disclosed, balancing public oversight with privacy protections.

## 8. Right to Respond
* **Inclusivity:** Whenever reasonably possible, institutions or individuals directly affected by a published audit should be given an opportunity to provide clarification or additional context before final publication or during subsequent revisions. This ensures the methodology remains balanced and multi-directional.

## 9. Principle of Intellectual Humility (The Keystone)
* **Definition:** *"We are not the owners of truth; we are the recorders of reality."*
* **Implementation:** The AAAP methodology seeks to document reality as accurately as possible based on the best available evidence at the time of publication. Audit records remain open to transparent revision whenever new, verifiable evidence becomes available. The objective is not to defend prior conclusions, but to preserve the integrity of the historical record.
