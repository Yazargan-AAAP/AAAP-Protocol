
# Security Policy

The security of the AAAP protocol is our top priority. We appreciate the community's help in identifying and reporting vulnerabilities so we can resolve them promptly.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you have discovered a potential security vulnerability, please **do not open a public issue.** Instead, report it privately to ensure we have time to address the issue before it is publicly disclosed.

Please send your report to: yazargan@proton.me 

### What to include in your report:
*   A clear description of the vulnerability.
*   Steps to reproduce the issue.
*   (Optional) Suggestions for potential fixes or mitigation strategies.

## Disclosure Policy
*   We will acknowledge receipt of your report as soon as possible.
*   We request that you keep the vulnerability confidential until we have had sufficient time to evaluate, patch, and release a fix.
*   Once a fix is deployed, we will credit the reporter in the release notes if they wish to be acknowledged.

Thank you for helping us keep the AAAP protocol secure.

# AAAP v2.x — Ethical Safeguard & Anti-Misuse Protocol

> *"The methodology must be stronger than the person using it."*

Whether wielded by an individual, a journalist, an artificial intelligence, or a massive public institution, the rules and ethical boundaries apply equally to all. 

## 1. Separation of Documentation and Accusation
* **Golden Rule:** *"Citizen Auditing documents evidence; it does not determine guilt."*
* **Application:** An auditor/citizen is neither a prosecutor nor a judge. Their role is to impartially seal events and digital footprints with UTC timestamps.

## 2. Physical Separation of Observation and Interpretation
* **Verified Observations:** Immutable, raw, and concrete data.
* **Interpretations (Optional):** Analytical evaluations built upon those observations.

## 3. Explicit Definition of Misuse
* The Citizen Auditing methodology can never be used for personal harassment, coordinated spam campaigns, misinformation, or fake documents.

## 4. Principle of Proportionality
* Not every discrepancy requires a global campaign. Direct issues to the correct counterpart/authority efficiently.

## 5. Audit and Limitation of Artificial Intelligence
* AI does not generate evidence; it organizes and structures it. Always follow the rule: *"Show your source."*

## 6. Transparent Correction Mechanism
* System errors are corrected transparently without concealing past records.

## 7. The Reciprocity Principle
* *"We apply the exact same ethical standards to institutions that we expect from citizens, and the exact same ethical standards to citizens that we expect from institutions."*
